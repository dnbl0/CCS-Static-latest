Cultural Collections

Unrivalled among Australian universities, our collections span visual arts, medical history, zoology, archives and Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural heritage.

Advanced Search

The University of Melbourne's cultural collections are unrivalled among Australian universities, spanning historical and contemporary collections across visual arts, cartography, medical history, zoology, archives and more, including significant collections of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander cultural heritage.

Browse collections

  • Grainger Museum Collection

    The personal collection of composer Percy Grainger including instruments, manuscripts, correspondence and experimental machines.

  • Harry Brookes Allen Museum of Anatomy and Pathology

    Anatomical and pathological specimens, models and teaching collections supporting medical education and research.

  • Henry Forman Atkinson Dental Museum

    Instruments, equipment and teaching materials documenting the history of dentistry and oral health education.

  • Medical History Museum

    Historical medical instruments, teaching models, pathological specimens and records documenting medical education.

  • University Art Collection

    A major university art collection featuring Australian and international works spanning painting, sculpture and works on paper.

Frequently Asked Questions

Having problems when searching for items across the cultural collections? Here are some frequently asked questions to help.

View all Frequently Asked Questions
How do I search across multiple collections at once?

By default, searches run across every collection in Cultural Collections Search. Use the filters on the results page to narrow a single collection, or to further refine your results by object type, access condition, or date.

What collections are currently included?

The platform is continuously being improved to include items from across the University's museums, archives, art, and scientific collections. Use Browse Collections to see the contributing collections. Please revisit frequently to discover new added items and collections.

Can I download images from my search results?

It depends on the item's copyright and terms of use. If a download icon appears near the preview window, you know the image, audio, or video can be used non-commercially. Respectful of the terms of use by always attributing the creator and the University of Melbourne.

Acknowledgement of Country

We acknowledge Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as the Traditional Owners of the unceded lands on which we work, learn and live. We pay respect to Elders past, present and future, and acknowledge the importance of Indigenous knowledge in the Academy.
