About Collections Navigator

What is Collections Navigator

Collections Navigator brings together the University of Melbourne’s rich and diverse cultural collections into a single discovery experience. It enables students, researchers, staff and the wider community to explore collections, uncover connections across disciplines, and engage with the University’s cultural heritage in new ways.

  • Collections Navigator is part of a broader vision to make the University’s collections more accessible and connected, supporting teaching, learning, research and public use.

The journey

  • Collections Navigator is being delivered progressively, with collections onboarding over time as systems, metadata and digital content are available.

  • The first phase brings together five collections across the University. More collections will be added over time, expanding the breadth and depth of content available through the platform.

  • As more collections join Collections Navigator, users will be able to search across an increasingly rich and diverse body of material, creating new opportunities for learning, research and innovation.

Collections Navigator is made possible with the support of the Myer Family

The development of Collections Navigator has been enabled by the generous support of the Myer family and the enduring legacy of the Sidney Myer University Trust.

  • For over a century, the Myer family has played a significant role in supporting education, research and cultural initiatives at the University of Melbourne. Their support of Collections Navigator helps ensure the University's cultural collections can be discovered, explored and shared by future generations.

How you can support this Initiative

Collections Navigator is an evolving initiative that relies on investment to expand and enhance access to the University's cultural collections.

  • Support for Collections Navigator helps:

    • Onboard additional collections to the platform
    • Digitise collection materials and increase online access
    • Enhance the quality and richness of collection metadata
    • Enhance search and research capabilities
    • Share the University's cultural heritage with current and future generations

  • If you are interested in supporting Collections Navigator, please contact the Museums & Collections team at MC-Collections@unimelb.edu.au to discuss partnership, sponsorship or philanthropic opportunities.

About Collections Navigator data

  • The collections found in Collections Navigator are in the care of the University of Melbourne. Documentation practices may vary between collections. Metadata and digital assets will continue to be enhanced over time as the platform evolves and additional collections are added.

  • Collections Navigator is an ongoing initiative. The volume and detail of accessible content will continue to grow as more collections become available to access through the platform.

Acknowledgement of Country

We acknowledge Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as the Traditional Owners of the unceded lands on which we work, learn and live. We pay respect to Elders past, present and future, and acknowledge the importance of Indigenous knowledge in the Academy.
