## Before you explore the collections

The University of Melbourne acknowledge Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as the Traditional Owners of the unceded lands on which we work, learn and live. We pay respect to Elders past, present and future, and acknowledge the importance of Indigenous knowledge in the Academy.

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people should be aware that the University of Melbourne website and collections contain the names, images and voices of people who have died.

Collections Navigator exposes longstanding and deep bias within collecting and descriptive practices. Users of this site are advised that some content, and the description of that content, is outmoded, racist, and culturally inappropriate and may cause distress. The University of Melbourne commits to identifying and addressing these issues, and also to being transparent about documenting such processes.
