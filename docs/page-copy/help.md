Choose a topic to find guidance on searching, accessing and using the University's cultural collections.

- **How do I search across multiple collections at once?**  
  By default, searches run across every collection in Collections Navigator. You can use filters on the results page to narrow to a specific collection, or to further refine your results by other metadata fields.
- **What collections are currently included?**  
  Collections Navigator is being delivered progressively, with collections onboarding over time as systems, metadata and digital content are available. The first phase brings together five collections across the University. More collections will be added over time, expanding the breadth and depth of content available through the platform. As more collections join Collections Navigator users will be able to search across an increasingly rich and diverse body of material, creating new opportunities for learning, research and innovation.
- **How do I request digital access to a collection item?**  
  Contact the relevant Collection Team for image requests. It makes the process easier if you include the item's persistent identifier and a description of your intended use.
- **How do I report any incorrect or incomplete metadata?**  
  We welcome community knowledge to help improve the accuracy of our records. Contact the Collection Team and include the item's persistent identifier to make the process easier.
- **What can I do if I have problems accessing the search?**  
  Try clearing your cache in the browser you are using. Browsers often save outdated information, which is the most common cause of access errors.

- **Note on images and voices**  
  Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people should be aware that the University of Melbourne website and collections contain the names, images and voices of people who have died.
- **Note on language**  
  Please be advised that people may be disturbed by the content of some collection items, which may include distressing images and descriptions, and derogatory terms used in their historical context, including for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people. They reflect the period in which they were created and are not the views of the University of Melbourne.
- **Note on terminology**  
  The following terms are used interchangeably throughout collection items: Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander, First Nations, First Peoples, Indigenous Australians (inclusive of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples), Aboriginal peoples and Indigenous peoples (commonly used for First Nations peoples internationally).
- **Contact**  
  If you have questions about Indigenous cultural materials, cultural protocols or access conditions, or wish to provide feedback about a record, please contact the relevant [collection team](#contact-us).

- **Free Access**  
  Collection items available for use for personal, research, educational or non-commercial purposes. Attribution to the creator and acknowledgement of University of Melbourne is required.
- **Licensed**  
  Some collection items are available for use under Creative Commons or other licence types. Please follow the specific licence terms shown on the individual record.
- **Cultural Conditions**  
  Where collection items carrying a cultural or advisory notice, engage respectfully with the content. For more information see [Indigenous Cultural Data and Access](/help/index.html#indigenous).
- **Contact the Collection**  
  To request permission to use collection items in publications, online and for commercial use, or for other specific enquiries, contact the relevant Collection. To assist in the query, note the information in Cite this item and prepare a description of your intended use.
- **University Copyright Office**  
  To learn more about copyright and licensing, visit the [University Copyright Office](https://copyright.unimelb.edu.au/).
